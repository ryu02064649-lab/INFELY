# Image sources

Originals kept for re-grading. The site uses the graded, cropped versions in `public/images/`.

- `dining-original.jpg` — DINING photo supplied by the owner. Cropped to 4:5 and graded to match the other service images.
- `stay-original.jpg` — STAY photo supplied by the owner. Cropped to 4:5 and graded to match.
- `experience-original.jpg` — EXPERIENCE photo supplied by the owner. Cropped to 4:5; graded a little lighter and warmer than the others (SAT 0.7, TARGET 0.18) so the evening sky keeps its glow.
- `gift-original.jpg` — GIFT photo supplied by the owner. Cropped to 4:5 around the bouquet and graded to match (SAT 0.5).
- `research-original.jpg` — RESEARCH photo supplied by the owner. Cropped to 4:5 (keeping the skyline) and graded to match (SAT 0.55).
- `hero-original.jpg` — Hero artwork supplied by the owner (emblem + wordmark). For the site the baked-in "RELY / PRIVATE RESEARCH & CONCIERGE" text was removed by inpainting (the page sets that text itself, animated) and the canvas was extended into darkness so the emblem sits above the copy at every screen size. The untouched original is also used for the OGP share image.
